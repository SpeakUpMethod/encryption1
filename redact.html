    How the Text tab works, and what to watch for

    The Text tab looks for details that follow a known pattern and checks them where the format allows, so ordinary numbers aren't mistaken for private ones.

    • What it finds. Email addresses; phone numbers (US, Mexico, Spain and international); card numbers (checked with the card checksum); bank accounts (IBAN and Mexican CLABE, checked); US Social Security numbers and ZIP codes; Mexican CURP and RFC; Spanish DNI and NIE (check letter verified); and IP addresses.
    • Same detail, same label. The same email or phone number gets the same label everywhere, so the text still makes sense: “[Name 1] will call [Name 2]”.
    • You decide. Untick anything that should stay. Nothing is hidden or kept without showing you first.

    What it can't do: It can't understand meaning, so it misses details written in words (“my birthday is the fifth of May”), addresses without a ZIP code, and names it wasn't given. It works on plain text; for Word or PDF files, copy the text in, or use Scrub to remove the file's hidden data.

    How Redact works, and what to watch for

    Redact never edits your original file. It draws the picture onto a blank canvas, paints solid boxes over it, and saves only those pixels as a new image. Then it opens the new file the way anyone receiving it would and checks it.

    • Solid boxes only. Blurring and pixelating can sometimes be reversed or guessed, especially for text and numbers, so Redact doesn’t offer them.
    • Cover generously. Include the whole word or number and a little margin; parts of letters at the edge can be enough to guess what was there. Check names in reflections, badges, screens and documents in the background too.
    • Hidden data is left out. Location, camera details, dates, author notes, preview images and extra pictures that phones sometimes attach are not copied into the new file. Redact lists what the original contained so you know what was removed.
    • Every save is checked. Redact confirms that every pixel inside every box is the box color in the saved file, that the rest of the picture is unchanged (PNG), that no hidden data or preview image is inside, and that the size is right.

    Limits. Redact works on images, not PDFs or documents: take a screenshot of the page first. Very large pictures are scaled down to about 16 megapixels, because some browsers can’t handle bigger ones; the page tells you when that happens. Anyone who already has the original still has everything in it.
