Create your own self-destrucing message

Burn guarantees a message or photo can only be retrieved once from the server, and the server never holds the decryption key. It cannot prevent someone from screenshotting, copying, or simply remembering what's shown once it's displayed on their screen — no technology can genuinely do that. Burn protects against the content existing indefinitely somewhere you don't control; it doesn't change what a person can do with what they've already been shown.
